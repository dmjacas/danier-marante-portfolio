# Technical Leadership

## Role

As a technical lead I am responsible for the technical outcome of the product, not just the code: architecture, decisions, delivery, quality and coordination.

## Responsibilities

- **Architecture** — solution design, frontend and backend architecture, cloud architecture.
- **Decisions** — technical decision making with explicit rationale and recorded trade-offs (see ADRs).
- **Coordination** — backend/frontend coordination across teams.
- **Delivery** — CI/CD and deployment coordination.
- **Quality** — code quality gates, automated tests, static analysis, code review culture.
- **Communication** — translating business requirements into technical work that the team can execute.

## How decisions are made

- Understand the business problem first.
- Evaluate realistic options, not just the familiar ones.
- Record the decision and its trade-offs (ADR pattern).
- Validate decisions in production through observability and metrics.
