# Team Practices

## Goal

Create conditions for a team to deliver good architecture reliably: shared standards, safe environments and clear accountability.

## Practices

- **Standards** — shared engineering standards and conventions; documented and applied to code review.
- **Code review** — review catches issues and transfers knowledge; focused on correctness, architecture intent and maintainability.
- **Mentoring** — support for less experienced engineers through review, pairing and clear expectations.
- **Backend/frontend coordination** — aligned contracts and cadence so integration happens continuously rather than at the end.
- **Decision making** — the team knows why decisions were made; technical decisions are discussable.
- **Quality culture** — automated tests, static analysis and quality gates are team-owned, not imposed.
- **Delivery** — working software, delivered incrementally, with clear acceptance criteria.
- **AI-assisted engineering** — tooling and practices evolve as tools evolve (GitHub Copilot since closed beta, Cursor, Windsurf, opencode), balanced with real review and judgment.
