## How decisions are documented

Every important architectural decision is documented:

1. **Context** — why the decision is being made.
2. **Problem** — the concrete problem to solve.
3. **Options** — realistic alternatives considered.
4. **Decision** — what was chosen.
5. **Trade-offs** — benefits and costs of the decision.
6. **Consequences** — what the team must now manage.

See [Architecture Decision Records](../docs/architecture-decisions/) for recorded examples.
